How it reads

Twelve pages opened for one business, the page that answered, and how a value comes to have a source.

The method

A fact is only worth what it is attached to.

We do not hand you a row of numbers and ask you to trust them. Every value is filed under the page it was read from, so you can go and look at it yourself. Below is the whole sequence, run once on one business — APEX ROOFING CO. in Phoenix, AZ — with what each move produced pinned beside it. Four moves, in order, four exhibits.

  1. 01

    Open the pages

    The register first, then the business's own site — contact, about, team, in that order. Six pages a site at most. A search result is never a source; a page is.

How to read one

A blank in the record is a finding, not a gap.

Four of the twelve are filled for every business without exception: the name, the trade you asked for, the market it trades in, and the date every claim on the record was tested. The other eight hold whatever the pages had to give, and a business that publishes nothing gets nothing in them. There are no dashes and no placeholders standing in for a fact nobody found — an empty field always has a page behind it that was opened, read, and came back with nothing. What it is not

An empty record is three things it is not.

  1. It is not we did not look.

    Twelve pages were opened for this business and every one of them is printed on this page. A business that had been skipped would have nothing to show here at all.

  2. It is not the business is gone.

    Its trading status on the state register reads Active. It trades, it files, and it simply does not publish a way to be reached — which is a fact about its website, not about its existence.

  3. It is not thrown away.

    The record sits in the file, it is inside the 148, and its reason for being empty is written beside it. Nothing was deleted to make the run look tidier than it was.

What it is worth

You still know it is there.

An empty record is not a dead row to be cleaned up. You know the business exists, you know it is part of the 148 in your market, and you know that a call to it would be a call nobody answered. That is the difference between a list of 148 businesses and a list of 91 contacts that quietly calls itself a market.
